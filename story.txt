The old lighthouse stood at the edge of the cliff, and the wind never stopped blowing around it. Every evening, the keeper climbed the narrow stairs to light the great lamp. He had done it for thirty years, and he knew every step by heart.

One night, a storm came from the sea. The waves were high, and the rain hit the windows like thousands of tiny stones. The keeper looked out into the darkness and saw a small boat. It was moving slowly, and the light of the lamp was the only hope for the people on board.

"Hold on!" he shouted, although nobody could hear him. He ran down the stairs, took a rope, and hurried to the shore. The water was cold, and the wind was strong, but he did not stop. He threw the rope, and a young sailor caught it.

Together, they pulled the boat to the rocks. The sailor was tired, but he smiled. "Thank you," he said. "The light saved us." The keeper only nodded. He was not a man of many words.

In the morning, the storm was gone. The sea was calm, and the sun rose over the water. The keeper sat on the stairs of the lighthouse and drank a cup of hot tea. Was it only a dream? No, the boat was still there, and the sailors were sleeping on the sand.

The keeper looked at the lamp and thought about the long years of his life. He was old, but the light was still strong. And the sea, as always, was waiting for the next night.
